# CueDeck (beta)

CueDeck is show-control software for theatre, concerts and events. Line up sounds, videos, pictures and announcements as cues, press **GO** (or a key, a MIDI pad, a phone, a Stream Deck) and it plays them. It works offline and is free to try during the beta.

This repository only holds the downloads and a place to report problems. The source code is private.

## Download

Get the newest version from the **[latest release page](https://github.com/revdad98/cuedeck-releases/releases/latest)**:

- **Mac:** `CueDeck-<version>-arm64.dmg`
- **Windows:** `CueDeck-Setup-<version>.exe`

## What you need

- **Mac:** macOS 12 or newer, Apple Silicon (M1 or later).
- **Windows:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

## First launch

CueDeck is a beta and is not code-signed yet, so your computer will warn you the first time. That is expected.

**Mac**
1. Open the downloaded `.dmg` and drag **CueDeck** into **Applications**.
2. Double-click CueDeck. macOS says it can't verify the app. Click **Done** (or **OK**).
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to CueDeck. Confirm with your password.
4. From now on it opens normally.

**Windows**
1. Double-click the downloaded `.exe`.
2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer. CueDeck appears in your Start menu.

## Updates

CueDeck checks for new versions by itself when it starts and shows an **Update** button when one is ready. Click it when you are not running a show; it downloads, checks the file, installs and reopens. You can also use **Settings → Updates → Check now**.

## Something went wrong?

Please tell us! Either:

- open the **[Issues tab](https://github.com/revdad98/cuedeck-releases/issues)** here and click **New issue**, or
- in CueDeck choose **Help → Report a problem**.

Please include your CueDeck version (shown in Help), whether you use Mac or Windows, and what you did just before it happened.

## Known limitations

- This is a **beta**: expect rough edges, and keep a backup of important shows (Save a show file).
- There is **no Intel Mac** build.
- The app is **not code-signed**, so the first-launch warnings above appear.

## Privacy

CueDeck has no accounts and no tracking. It only contacts GitHub to check for updates and, if you choose to download voices for announcements, to download those voice packs. Your shows and sound files stay on your computer.
